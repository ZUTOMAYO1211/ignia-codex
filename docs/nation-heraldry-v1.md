## 최종 생성 프롬프트

Design an original fantasy heraldic emblem concept sheet for NINE distinct fictional nations/polities in the Ignia world. Exactly 3 columns x 3 rows on clean white background, identical cell size, generous spacing and margins. No text, labels, numbers or lettering. Modern flat heraldic insignia, crisp bold vector-like geometric shapes, charcoal monochrome symbols on colored shield or seal fields, limited 2-3 flat colors each. No gradients, lighting, shadows, metal effects, filigree or photorealism. Strong readable silhouette at small sizes, more heraldic than app icons; unify weight and scale while differentiating outer shield/seal geometry. All original motifs, do not reproduce any game or real-world national insignia. Reading order: Row1 col1 Lumen kingdom, order justice and magic purification: ivory/soft gold shield featuring upright faceted purification crystal and balanced pair of wings or symmetric ramparts; row1 col2 Karnix empire, meritocratic conquest and strength: muted crimson angular shield, dominant upward spearhead above three converging steel-like chevrons; row1 col3 Orgen elevated trading city, alchemy invention commerce: pale cobalt octagonal seal with simple cog surrounding a suspended bridge arch. Row2 col1 Slag lower canyon city, survival freedom and unregulated alchemy: muted acidic sage asymmetric-bottom shield containing cracked flask and two simple smoke curves; row2 col2 Silvaria elf autonomy, forest and spirits: soft mint pointed oval seal with branching tree whose roots form a protective ring; row2 col3 Kazdum dwarf clan union, mountains and craftsmanship: warm sand hexagonal shield, stylized anvil beneath mountain peak with two crossed compact hammers. Row3 col1 Arcanum neutral sorcerer academy, research freedom stellar origins: lilac circular seal with four-point star nested in orbital armillary arcs over an open-book shape; row3 col2 High Tide free union, island autonomy sea trade and captains: light turquoise round seal, three sails rising from a single wave with small compass point; row3 col3 Frimhar northern tribal league, ice survival and resistance: icy blue notched shield, symmetrical two frost fangs around a compact snowflake. Keep symbols simple, coherent professional heraldic icon family. Nine emblems only, no tenth emblem. These are draft interpretations of lore, not finalized canon.
